# TindaHalin - Plain Language Privacy Notice

## Data Collection & Usage
- TindaHalin operates entirely **on your device**.
- Customer names and phone numbers are **optional** and only used for local credit ("utang") management.
- Anonymous cash buyers leave zero personal identifiable data.
- The app does not access contacts, location, microphone, or SMS contents.
- Backup files are created locally and under your control.
